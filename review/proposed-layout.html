About

Hi, I'm Mitchell. I am a software engineer working across cloud infrastructure, platform engineering, and DevOps, with a background in systems and storage research. My work spans high‑performance systems programming, storage architectures, and sustainability‑driven design, with a focus on efficiency, scalability, and environmental impact.

I earned my M.S. in Computer Science from the University of California, Santa Cruz, where I worked with the Center for Research in Systems and Storage (CRSS). My research explored hybrid storage architectures, life cycle assessment (LCA), and simulation‑based approaches to evaluating and optimizing data center design across cost, performance, and sustainability metrics.

In addition to my research, I served as the Head Teaching Assistant for CSE 130: Principles of Computer System Design across six quarters for a course serving over 1,000 students. I led discussion sections, created assignments and quizzes, managed course infrastructure, and developed automated grading workflows to improve efficiency and consistency in evaluating programming assignments. GitLab CI/CD handled submission checks, while full functional grading ran separately in Docker on an Ubuntu VM.

Beyond academia, I’ve worked across full‑stack development, DevOps, and infrastructure automation. I’ve built CI/CD pipelines, Kubernetes monitoring solutions, and workflow integrations that improve operational reliability at scale. I approach every project with a collaborative mindset and a commitment to measurable outcomes, whether optimizing infrastructure for performance, designing cost‑effective storage systems, or integrating sustainability into engineering workflows.
